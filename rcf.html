Chapter 4 · Rules of the Air

Radio Failure in IMC

What an IFR flight does after a two-way radio communication failure in instrument conditions (ICAO Annex 2 / PANS-ATM, as followed in India).

0.0 s

Steps

    Remember

    • Squawk 7600
    • Keep last assigned speed and level (or minimum flight altitude if higher) for 20 min in procedural airspace / 7 min in radar airspace
    • Then fly the filed flight plan level, speed and route
    • Descend at EAT (or ETA if no EAT), fly a normal instrument approach
    • Land within 30 min after ETA or last acknowledged EAT, whichever is later
