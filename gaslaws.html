Gas Laws in the Body

As the cabin altitude rises, the surrounding pressure falls. Watch three gas laws at work in the body at the same time.

0.0 s

Steps

    Remember

    • Boyle – trapped gas expands as pressure falls (ears, sinuses, teeth, gut)
    • Dalton – oxygen is always 21%, but its partial pressure falls with total pressure → hypoxia
    • Henry – less pressure, less gas stays dissolved → nitrogen bubbles → decompression sickness
    • Graham – gases diffuse from high to low partial pressure (lungs → blood → tissues); Charles – pressure ∝ temperature
