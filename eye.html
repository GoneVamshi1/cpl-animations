The Eye

Cross-section of the human eye. Light passes through the cornea, pupil and lens and is focused on the retina; the fovea gives sharp central (cone) vision, the periphery is covered by rods.

0.0 s

Steps

    Remember

    • Cornea does 70–80% of the focusing; the lens fine-focuses (accommodation)
    • Fovea – only cones: sharp day and colour vision, NO night vision
    • Rods in the periphery – black & white, very sensitive in dim light, detect movement; full dark adaptation about 30 min
    • Blind spot – where the optic nerve leaves; no receptors (the other eye covers it)
