Freedoms of the Air

Each panel shows an airline of the home State (India) and what it may do in a foreign State. Freedoms 1–2 are technical, 3–5 commercial (Chicago 1944); 6–9 are not in any convention.

Steps

    Remember

    • 1st and 2nd freedoms – Transit Agreement 1944 (ratified by India)
    • 3rd, 4th and 5th – Air Transport Agreement 1944 (not ratified by India)
    • 6th = 3rd + 4th through the home State
    • 8th and 9th are cabotage – carriage inside a foreign State
