Chapter 4 · Rules of the Air

Interception Signals

Visual signals between an intercepting (military) aircraft and the intercepted civil aircraft – Series 1, 2 and 3 of ICAO Annex 2, Appendix 1/2.

0.0 s

Steps

    Remember

    • Series 1 – interceptor slightly above, ahead and normally to the LEFT rocks wings and flashes nav lights, then a slow level turn (normally left): 'You have been intercepted – follow me'
    • Series 2 – abrupt break-away, climbing turn of 90° or more without crossing your line of flight: 'You may proceed'
    • Series 3 – gear down, steady landing lights, overflies the runway: 'Land at this aerodrome'
    • Intercepted aircraft acknowledges: rock wings and follow (gear down for Series 3); squawk 7700 and call on 121.5 MHz
